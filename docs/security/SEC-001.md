# SEC-001 — Proposed privacy controls and threat model

**Status: PROPOSED; human decisions and implementation evidence pending.**
This addresses [issue #3](https://github.com/thakurshadman/quran-hifz/issues/3).
It is a reviewable specification, not approval to recruit, collect, transfer,
train on, or publish participant data. No runtime controls have been implemented
or tested. SEC-001 stays open until its acceptance evidence is recorded.

Read [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md), the [PRD](../product/PRD.md),
[NFR-001](../requirements/NFR-001.md), and the
[ASR plan](../research/ASR-FEASIBILITY-PLAN.md). Existing requirements remain
unchanged. New controls, deadlines, and study restrictions below are proposals
requiring human approval; approval of this document alone does not authorize a
particular experiment. No provider, storage service, or architecture is selected.

## Assets and trust boundaries

Voice can identify a speaker and reveal religious practice, background speech,
or bystanders. Random participant IDs are pseudonyms, not anonymization. Protect
recordings, transcripts, word timings/error annotations, embeddings/features,
speaker manifests, consent/contact mappings, withdrawal records, credentials,
and unpublished results. Authoritative Qur’an text, prompt provenance/timings,
and feedback integrity are separately protected assets.

| Candidate flow | Boundary and exposure to assess before approval |
| --- | --- |
| Local processing | Person/microphone → browser or native capture buffers → model → alignment/UI. Inspect memory, browser storage, caches, crash reports, OS temporary files and model downloads; local does not mean exposure-free. |
| Remote processing | Capture buffers → client/network → service or proxy → speech provider/subprocessors → results. Document every recipient, region, retention/training policy and deletion mechanism; responses remain untrusted. |
| Restricted research | Consented capture/import → approved encrypted store → named annotators/evaluation workers → restricted derived artifacts → reviewed aggregate publication. Consent/contact mapping is separate from pseudonymous data; job copies and backups are in scope. |
| Repository and CI | Maintainer/dependency/model source → reviewed commits and locked artifacts → CI/evaluation runner. Public git, PRs, issues, logs and CI artifacts must never receive private recordings, transcripts, participant manifests, or secrets. |

Each experiment must draw its actual flows, including temporary copies and
administrative access, and identify which candidate boundaries apply. A new flow
or recipient requires renewed review before data crosses it.

and experiments must reassess likelihood, impact and residual risk. All listed

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEC-T01 | Hidden/stale microphone capture or uncancelled upload leaks speech; prompt audio is mistaken for the participant. | SEC-C01/C02; SEC-V01/V02 | Approve capture/prompt isolation design and measurable buffer limits. |
| SEC-T02 | Intercepted transport, exposed key, broad storage access or compromised worker exfiltrates identifiable voice. | SEC-C03/C04/C06/C07; SEC-V03/V04/V06/V07 | Approve storage, access roster and actual local/remote boundaries. |

## Proposed retention and withdrawal protocol

The following deadlines are recommendations for the first study, not current
service promises. The human owner must accept or revise them before recruitment;
the participant notice must then match exactly. Start with self-attested adults
18+ only. Do not collect birth dates, identity documents or extra voice samples
to verify age/identity; stop and escalate suspected ineligible participation.
Minor participation needs a separately reviewed protocol.

No participant-data model training/fine-tuning, speaker identification, public
recording redistribution, or unrelated reuse is authorized. The ASR plan's
train/tune/test split does not override consent: candidate evaluation and declared
parameter tuning are distinct from fitting model weights. Training would require
a separate proposal, rights review and new consent addressing withdrawal limits.

| Data class | Proposed lifetime and handling |
| --- | --- |
| Research audio, transcripts, timings, annotations, features/embeddings, per-speaker results and manifests | Delete at the earlier of 90 days after collection or 30 days after the experiment closes, unless withdrawn sooner. No automatic renewal; continued retention needs renewed explicit consent before expiry. |
| Worker/browser caches, temporary exports and processing copies | Clear on completion/cancellation/error; bound any crash leftovers to 24 hours. These research copy limits do not set the still-TBD ordinary transient audio buffer limit. |
| Contact-to-participant mapping and consent record | Store separately with minimal contact method, random withdrawal code, consent version/time and approved scope. Delete contact mapping when deletion is confirmed; retain minimal consent/deletion evidence for at most 90 days after final data deletion, then purge. No legal-hold exception is assumed. |
| Sanitized operational logs and access audit records | Keep at most 30 days; access audits may identify authorized staff, but must not contain participant identity or voice/text payloads. Restrict audit access. |
| Backups/object versions/provider copies | Prefer research storage without participant-data backups. If backups exist, require expiry/deletion within 30 days after active deletion; block access/reuse immediately and suppress deleted records before any restore is exposed. Provider behavior must meet the same limits or the candidate is excluded. |
| Public aggregate reports | Release only after disclosure review removes participant-level rows, identifying references and small/cohort combinations that permit identification. Genuinely non-identifying published aggregates may remain; disclosure review and this limit must appear in consent. |

Withdrawal workflow, proposed:

1. Participant uses the approved private contact and random withdrawal code.
   The data steward verifies using the existing consent mapping; do not demand
   new identity documents/audio. If the code is lost, use the existing private
   contact record where possible and escalate ambiguity without disclosing data.
2. Acknowledge within 3 calendar days. Block further use/transfer immediately on
   receipt, including scheduled benchmark jobs; isolate ambiguous records while
   resolving identity. Track receipt time and affected restricted artifact IDs.
3. Delete active source/derived copies and request downstream deletion within
   7 calendar days of receipt. Verify providers, exports and failed jobs; retry
   failures and escalate to the incident lead. Never mark an unverified deletion
   complete or make an unsupported participant promise.
4. A frozen benchmark does not exempt a recording. Retire affected manifests,
   remove participant-linked artifacts, issue a new dataset version and rerun
   affected comparisons. Keep only sanitized version/change metadata publicly;
   mark obsolete results as superseded without identifying who withdrew.
5. Confirm active deletion privately and state any pending backup/provider expiry
   and its deadline. Final confirmation follows verified deletion of all retained
   copies (including backups within 30 days of active deletion). Maintain a
   restricted suppression list only through backup expiry/restore verification,
   then purge its participant links. Public non-identifying aggregates cannot
   generally be recalled; no identifiable raw data may be published under this plan.

The data steward owns the copy inventory and deletion queue; the security lead
handles failures and accidental disclosures. Both roles remain unassigned.

## Participant notice readiness

Before recruitment, fill and independently review the
[participant notice template](RESEARCH-CONSENT-TEMPLATE.md). Supply the actual
study owner/contact, study purpose/tasks, duration, approved storage/location,
named recipient organizations, local/remote path, retention dates, withdrawal
route and risks. Obtain a voluntary affirmative consent record before capture;
microphone browser permission alone is not consent to research retention.
Reject blank/unapproved fields. Give the participant a copy and withdrawal code.
Changed purpose, recipients or retention require new consent before use.

Avoid bystander capture; explain how to stop and report accidental background
speech, and quarantine/delete such recordings pending review. Participation is
optional; declining/stopping does not incur a penalty. Do not promise anonymous
voice, flawless deletion from an unverified provider, or certified tajwīd feedback.

## Private reporting and incident handoff

Read-only repository inspection for this draft returned
`GET /repos/thakurshadman/quran-hifz/private-vulnerability-reporting` →
`enabled: false`. A subsequent enablement request (`PUT` to that endpoint)
returned HTTP 403, `Resource not accessible by integration`; reporting remains
disabled. These are point-in-time observations, not evidence of activation.
The repository owner can open
[security settings](https://github.com/thakurshadman/quran-hifz/settings/security_analysis),
find **Private vulnerability reporting**, and choose **Enable**. A permitted
maintainer must then verify API state and the private report route under
**Security → Advisories → Report a vulnerability**, and verify the appointed
recipient can receive/triage a synthetic, non-sensitive rehearsal report.
Neither enablement, monitoring nor rehearsal is claimed complete.

Until verified, publicly request only that the owner establish a private channel;
do not post exploit details, tokens, audio or participant information. No security
email address or monitored response SLA is currently designated. Participant
withdrawal contact is a separate SEC-D03 decision; GitHub advisories are not a
substitute for an accessible participant contact.

Proposed incident runbook: the named lead privately triages severity and affected
flows; pauses capture/transfers/jobs; revokes exposed access/credentials; restricts
minimal evidence; inventories affected participants/copies; coordinates fixes,
deletion and safe recovery; and approves resumption after verification. The human
owner and lead determine applicable notification obligations and participant
communication before making promises; no jurisdiction or legal deadline is
assumed. Rehearse reporting, containment, withdrawal during outage and restore
suppression with synthetic records. Publish only a sanitized incident summary.
