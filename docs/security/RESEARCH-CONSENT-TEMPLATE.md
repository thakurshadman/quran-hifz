## Information to give the participant

We invite adults aged 18 or older to a Qur’an memorization research study run by
[responsible person/organization], privately reachable at [approved contact].
The purpose is to evaluate how accurately and quickly software tracks recitation;
it does not certify tajwīd. You will [approved tasks, passages and session length].
Taking part is voluntary. You may decline or stop without penalty.

We will collect [exact audio, transcript, annotation and minimal cohort fields].
Your voice may identify you and reveal religious practice or background speech.
We use a random participant code, but this does not make your voice anonymous.
Please avoid recording other people; stop and contact us if this happens.

Processing occurs [approved local/remote path]. [Named organizations/roles] can
access [specific data] in [approved storage and region]; [named providers and
subprocessors, or explicitly none] receive [specific data] under [verified terms].
We do not use these recordings to train/fine-tune models, identify speakers,
publish raw recordings or transcripts, or for unrelated purposes. Publication is
limited to reviewed non-identifying aggregate results. Risks include unauthorized
access and mistaken feedback; [actual controls] reduce but cannot eliminate them.

Research recordings and linked derived data are deleted by the earlier of
90 days after collection or 30 days after experiment closure: [concrete expiry
rule/date and closure date if known]. Temporary copies clear after processing;
crash leftovers expire within 24 hours. Sanitized logs expire within 30 days.
Minimal consent/deletion evidence expires within 90 days after final deletion.
[Describe actual backup/provider behavior and expiry, within approved limits.]

To withdraw, contact [approved private route] with your withdrawal code. We
acknowledge within 3 calendar days, block further use when received, and delete
active copies within 7 calendar days. Backups/provider copies must be deleted
within 30 days of active deletion; we confirm what is complete and what remains
pending. Withdrawal removes your data from future benchmarks, including frozen
sets. Already published non-identifying aggregate results may remain. If you lose
the code, contact us through your existing contact method; we will help without
asking for identity documents or additional voice samples.

## Affirmative consent record

Record privately: random participant code, notice version, date/time, consent
response, and minimal approved contact route stored separately from recordings.
Provide a copy of the notice and code. Do not collect a legal name, signature
image, birth date or identity document solely for this template.

- I confirm I am at least 18 years old.
- I have read this notice, could ask questions, and understand what is recorded,
  where it goes, how long it is kept and how to withdraw.
- I voluntarily agree to the described recording and restricted evaluation use.

All responses must be affirmative before capture. Browser microphone permission
does not replace this record. Re-consent is required before any newly approved
purpose, recipient or extended retention; silence is not agreement.
